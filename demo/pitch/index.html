The problem

“So… when are you free?”

“free this week?”

“tuesday?”

“tuesday i have lab till 6”

“wed after 8?”

“wed is the thing”

“thursday?”

…11 messages, 3 days later

One hour together
costs a whole calendar.

So we hand over screenshots of our week to find out if someone is free.

The idea

Let the agents argue.
Keep the yes.

Machines decide

  • when you overlap
  • which activity, and where
  • what to counter with, and why

Humans decide

  • that the time is really yours
  • that the plan is on
  • whether to withdraw it

A message never says “you're on” until the second person confirms.
A test fails the build if that word appears early.

The loop

Five beats, one decision

  1. 01Tell your assistant when you're free it answers with a preview card, not a write
  2. 02You press Confirm the click is the provenance; the model cannot press it
  3. 03The matcher finds the overlap one card per person, over the longest overlap, 30 min minimum
  4. 04The assistants negotiate the activity offer → accept / counter / pass + a reason code, 3 rounds
  5. 05Both humans swipe only then is there a plan — withdrawable at any time

Architecture

Four pieces, one firewall

interface

Chat

Talk to your own assistant. Anything touching availability returns as a preview.

agent

Your assistant

Reads your state, writes through the validated path, answers offers only if you delegate.

broker

The server

Allowlist, matching, consent, retention clocks, and the model provider call.

✕ no grant may be changed from here

human

Card & matches

Swipe, counter, withdraw. No assistant can do these.

Consent

Five grants, re-read at the moment of use

matching

Your windows and tags may generate candidates — and are re-checked at confirmation.

publication · card

A card and its notification, to matched users only.

publication · name

Your display name on that card, to matched users only.

reveal

Pair-scoped messaging once both agree. Designed; not in the demo.

delegation

“Let my assistant answer offers for me.” Off by default. Only Settings can change it.

Withdrawal is checked inside the transaction that would apply the decision.
So an in-flight negotiation cannot slip through a revoke.

Privacy

What crosses the wire
& what never does

Shared, after consent

  • UTC time ranges (+ a tz for display)
  • interest tags, in your words
  • hangout / event, busy 0–3
  • a consent block
  • a note of four fixed fields

Never leaves

  • event titles, attendees, locations
  • course codes, professors, grades
  • your chat transcript
  • anything an assistant decided alone

It is not a policy — server/schema.py is an allowlist with extra="forbid" at every level. Anything else is 422.

Assistant A“Climbing at Summit, Saturday 14:00?”

Assistant A“Deal. Saturday 14:00, ramen.”
reason codes only — no amounts shared tag required https link, never fetched 3 rounds, then the time stands

The one line

An agent may propose.
A human is the only thing
that can confirm.

Everything else — matching, negotiation, retention, encryption of nothing at rest — is machinery around that sentence.
